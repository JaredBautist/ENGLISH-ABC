# Requirements

Approved scope: replace only the content of `jardin-hello` with the ten-slide visual/listening sequence discussed with the user. Preserve the approved UI, existing images and Google-backed speech integration.

- WHEN the unit opens, it SHALL provide greeting, vocabulary, three teacher-narrated Teddy scenes, the existing song, two visual choices, an echo game and home practice.
- WHEN children participate, they SHALL be able to point, gesture or speak without required reading or writing.
- WHEN existing speech controls are activated, their content SHALL be short English phrases; Spanish facilitation instructions SHALL remain in descriptions, not spoken fields.
- WHEN choices appear, both options SHALL resolve to existing, distinct greeting/farewell images; correct answers SHALL occupy different positions.
- WHEN this unit changes, all other unit values/order, routes, UI components and speech services SHALL remain unchanged.
- WHEN home practice is offered, it SHALL accept a caregiver and voluntary gestures without recordings, writing or mandatory purchases.

Acceptance checks: unit sequence and assets, supported schema, choice mappings, real component-to-speech integration with mocked audio, complete suite/build and before/after catalogue comparison.
