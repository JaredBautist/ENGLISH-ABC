# Jardín — Hello! / Teacher guide

## Purpose and references

Original adaptation of sections 5, 6 and 8 of [the pedagogical proposal](PROPUESTA_PARA_DEBATIR.md). This is not an official MEN lesson. Children participate through observation, listening, gesture and optional speech; no independent reading or writing is required.

## Preparation

Open Jardín, unit 1. Check sound and the existing video before class. No purchased toy is needed: the displayed teddy represents Teddy. The teacher acts out arrival and departure because the reused teddy illustration is static. Model the farewell image explicitly; a wave alone can mean either greeting or goodbye.

Use short moments across sessions according to the group. The ten slides are not a mandatory single-session schedule.

## Facilitation

| Slide | Adult action | Child participation |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hello! | Play the card; model a greeting. | Wave, look or say Hello. |
| 2. Hello / Bye-bye | Act out entering and leaving; play each card. | Point to the scene or imitate. |
| 3. Hello, Teddy! | Introduce Teddy as arriving; play the phrase. | Greet Teddy. |
| 4. My name is Teddy. | Play Teddy's introduction; model your own name. | Optionally say their name or point to themselves. |
| 5. Bye-bye, Teddy! | Act out Teddy leaving; play the phrase. | Say goodbye or gesture. |
| 6. Song | Preview first; invite a wave on Hello. | Listen and optionally join in. |
| 7–8. Listen and point | Say “Escucha y señala la imagen”; press the question speaker, then select what the child points to. | Match the heard greeting/farewell to its modeled scene. |
| 9. Echo | Play/say one phrase at a time, leaving a pause. | Echo at a comfortable volume or gesture. |
| 10. Home practice | Explain arriving, saying a name and leaving to a caregiver. | Reuse the three modeled phrases or gestures. |

Do not require children to read the option labels. The speaker icon on an answer card is part of the existing UI: that card selects an answer; the question speaker plays the prompt. Existing incorrect-answer feedback reveals the solution, so these are guided practice, not an independent assessment.

## Observation and support

Observe whether the child distinguishes greeting/farewell in the modeled context, responds to a greeting and participates in introductions. Note “with modeling / with support / independently” separately from presentation completion. Silence is not automatically failure. Accept pointing and accessible gestures; never require jumping, touch or recording.

Reduce to one modeled phrase if needed. Extend by repeating the story with another participant, not by introducing a large new vocabulary list. The echo is a rhythm/listening activity, not a formal phonics lesson.

## Offline and audio limits

Teacher narration replaces unavailable video or speech. Google-backed speech is reused unchanged; Spanish guidance remains in descriptions, while clickable speech fields contain English. Existing browser fallback depends on installed voices; it is not a guarantee of complete offline operation.

Live playback, current video suitability and classroom effectiveness require teacher verification. No new student recordings or personal data are collected.
